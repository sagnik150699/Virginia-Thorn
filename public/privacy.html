Cookies & Your Data

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This page explains, in plain English, what happens to your information when you visit this website and the choices you have. The short version: there are no accounts, the site does no tracking of its own, visit statistics can be switched off at any time, and the videos are played through YouTube.

Who is responsible for your information

This website belongs to Virginia Thorn, an actor, singer and voice-over artist based in London and East Sussex. Virginia is responsible for any personal information collected through it (in data protection terms, she is the "controller").

If you have a question about this policy, or a request about your information, please get in touch through the contact page or through Virginia's Spotlight profile.

What this site collects

There are no accounts, logins, newsletters or online shop on this site, and it does not run any tracking of its own. You can read every page without telling anyone who you are.

The only information gathered while you browse is the standard access logs kept by the hosting service and, if analytics is in use, visit statistics from Google Analytics, which you can switch off at any time. Both are explained below.

When you get in touch

If you contact Virginia through the contact page or through Spotlight, the details you share, such as your name, email address and message, are used only to reply to you and to deal with your enquiry or booking. They are kept only for as long as that takes.

The form on the contact page is currently a design mock-up: it does not send or store anything you type into it. When it goes live, this policy will be updated to explain how messages sent through it are handled.

Messages you send through Spotlight are also covered by Spotlight's own privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Firebase Hosting, a service run by Google. Like almost every website host, Google's servers keep standard access logs whenever a page is requested. These include your IP address, the type of browser and device you are using, the page you asked for and the time. The logs are used to keep the site secure and running properly.

You can read how Google looks after this information in Privacy and Security in Firebase and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Google may process this information on servers outside the UK. When it does, it protects it with the standard safeguards that UK data protection law requires for international transfers.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

To understand which pages people visit and how the site could be better, the site may use Google Analytics 4, a service from Google. When it is switched on, it records things like:

  • the pages you view and the website that sent you here;
  • your approximate location, such as your country or town, worked out from your IP address;
  • your type of device and browser.

Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses, and the site tells Google not to use the data for advertising or personalised ads. The reports Virginia sees show totals and trends, not who you are. Detailed records are deleted automatically after the retention period set in Google Analytics, which is never more than 14 months.

You can switch analytics off at any time. Press "Reject" in the cookie banner, or choose Cookie settings at the bottom of any page and press "Reject" there. The analytics script then stops loading and its cookies are removed from this site. You can turn it back on the same way with "Accept".

Google explains what it does with this information in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Embedded YouTube videos

The Reels and Music pages include videos hosted on YouTube, which is owned by Google. They are embedded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which YouTube says does not store information about you just because a video appears on the page.

Once you press play, YouTube may set cookies and collect information about how you watch, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. The cookie banner on this site controls the visit statistics only: YouTube's own cookies are managed by YouTube, and you can block or delete them in your browser settings.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. These are the ones this site, Google Analytics and YouTube may use:

Name Set by What it does How long it lasts
vt-cookie-consent This site Remembers whether you pressed Accept or Reject, so the banner does not ask again on every page. The same choice is also kept in your browser's local storage under the same name. 12 months
_ga Google Analytics Holds a random ID so that Google Analytics can tell one browser from another and count visits. Only set while analytics is switched on. 2 years
_ga_<id> Google Analytics Keeps track of the current visit, so pages viewed together count as one visit. The ending is the site's Google Analytics ID. Only set while analytics is switched on. 2 years
YouTube cookies YouTube (Google) Set by YouTube when you play an embedded video, for example to run the player, count views and protect against abuse. Set by YouTube; see Google's Privacy Policy

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them; you may just see the cookie banner again.

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, including Spotlight, IMDb, YouTube, Instagram, X (formerly Twitter), Bandcamp and the websites of theatre companies Virginia works with. When you follow one of those links you leave this site, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you have the right to:

  • Access: ask for a copy of the personal information held about you.
  • Rectification: ask for it to be corrected if it is wrong or incomplete.
  • Erasure: ask for it to be deleted.
  • Restriction: ask for its use to be limited.
  • Objection: object to it being used.
  • Portability: ask for it in a format you can take elsewhere.
  • Withdrawing consent: change your mind at any time about anything you agreed to, for example by choosing "Cookie settings" and pressing "Reject".

To use any of these rights, get in touch through the contact page or Spotlight. You will normally get a reply within one month, and there is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk. Virginia would be grateful for the chance to put things right first, so please do get in touch.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect any information from them. If you think a child has shared personal information through the site, please get in touch and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated whenever the way the site handles information changes, for example when the contact form goes live or a new service is added. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
